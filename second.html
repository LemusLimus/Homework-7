My Short Story

Once upon a time, there were 2 men sitting in a pub, drinking their hearts out to eachother. Then, into the bar, came a gunslinger who would never say a word.

The two men halted their conversation when the man sat. They scowled and looked him up and down, a rusty revolver hung from his hip, the iron stained with a red substance.

He didn't utter a word, simply raised his hand and a glass slid across the bar. The wind seemed to follow the gunslinger inside, his battered scarf swaying in the breeze only he could feel.

The two men stare in silence at the wanted poster on the wall. It depicted a ragged man with stitched mouth and a cocked hat, they called him,"The Man with the Scarcrow's Face"
